Read widely. Practise critical thinking. Build judgment in the age of AI.

02 / Why it matters

AI can give you an answer in seconds.

The harder work is deciding what to ask, which sources to trust and what you think.

AI can suggest and challenge. You check the evidence, make the judgment and own the work.

06 / Before you bring your question

Common questions.

?
01Do I need to know what I want to study?

No. Begin with a question, book, topic or problem that has your attention. If subject or university choices are coming up, we can explore those alongside it.

02What does AI have to do with this?

AI can help you explore and challenge an idea. You will still read, check sources, compare perspectives and decide what you think.

03Does this help with university admissions?

It can. An interest you have explored seriously may help you make course choices and speak about your work in essays or interviews. We can connect the work to Indian or overseas university decisions when those are relevant to your stage.

04What if my interests keep changing?

That is normal. You do not need to force one interest into a lifelong plan. We look for patterns in what you enjoy reading, questioning and making, then help you choose one useful direction to explore next.

05What happens after the form is submitted?

We'll review what the parent or legal guardian shares and contact them by phone if a conversation would be useful. We would begin with the student's question and current stage.
